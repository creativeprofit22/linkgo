# 11 — Evidence Audit And Synthesis

Status: **outline — not started.** Sections below say what each part must
contain. Nothing here is a finding yet.

| Phase       | 11 of 11                                                                                        |

Modelled on the reference study's cross-reference audit.

---

## 1. Freshness Check

> Sampling rule, URLs re-fetched, how many are still live and unchanged, and
> any claims affected.

## 2. Claim Audit Table

> Every material claim from Phases 2–10: claim, source phase, evidence,
> verdict (Supported / Partially supported / Unproven / Contradicted), and the
> required action.

## 3. Final Hypothesis Verdicts

> H1–H12: final verdict, confidence, key evidence for and against, and how the
> verdict changed from earlier phases.

## 4. What The Evidence Says Overall

> Plain summary of the market, audience, competition, legal risk, and size,
> including findings that contradict the owner's notes.

## 5. Assumption Register

> Remaining assumptions: assumption, evidence, confidence, test, owner, date.

## 6. What Public Data Cannot Prove

> Questions needing direct research (interviews, surveys, pilots, retention
> and spend tests), each with a proposed method, sample size, and decision
> rule, adapted from the reference study's validation tools.

## 7. Red-Team Review

> The Disconfirmer's challenge to the final report and how each point was
> resolved.

## 8. Verifier Reports

> Both Verifiers' results and the resulting edits.

## 9. Research Totals

> Total requests, spend against the cap, raw data locations, and how to rerun
> the study.
