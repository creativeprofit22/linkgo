---

## 1. Enforcement History

> Dated takedowns, cease-and-desist letters, and lawsuits against fan games,
> ports, mods, and hosting sites, by rights holder and project type.

## 2. Rights-Holder Content Policies

> Published fan-content and modding guidelines from major rights holders, with
> snapshot dates.

## 3. Dormant IP

> What "dormant" means legally (usually still owned), and documented cases of
> revivals: licensed, tolerated, or shut down.

## 4. How Port Projects Manage Risk

> Patterns such as requiring the user's own original files, and how they have
> fared in enforcement history.

## 5. Platform And Hosting Terms

> Hub, store, and code-host terms relevant to hosting mods, ports, and fan
> games, and their takedown processes.

## 6. Minors And Community Safety

> Obligations and risks from a likely under-18 audience: platform age rules,
> Discord policies, data-protection rules for children, and moderation needs.

## 7. Risk Register

> Numbered risks with likelihood, impact, evidence, and a question for the
> lawyer.

## 8. Disconfirming Evidence

> Tolerated or licensed fan projects and successful revivals.

## 9. Verdict

> H7 with cited evidence and confidence, plus the overall legal risk level.

## 10. Verifier Report

> Claims checked, confirmed, dropped, downgraded, and resulting edits.

## 11. Run Log And Spend

> Request counts by command, spend against allocation, raw-file locations.
