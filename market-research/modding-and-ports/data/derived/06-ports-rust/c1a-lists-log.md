# C1a — curated-list port inventory log (tier = listed)

Output: `port-inventory-listed.csv`. It is rebuilt with `scripts/c1a-build-listed.cjs scripts/c1a-pcgw-parsed.json scripts/c1a-resetera-parsed.json`. The parse helpers are `scripts/c1a-parse-pcgw.cjs` and `scripts/c1a-parse-resetera.cjs`. They were run on link-stripped temp text taken from the stored envelopes.
Inputs are stored records only. No network calls were made and nothing was spent. URLs and personal usernames are removed. Unnamed projects are written as `[decomp project]` or `[unnamed port]`.

## Caveats

- The lists use different platform labels (PCGW "Windows"/"Phone"/"Engines" became PC, Mobile and unclear). "PC" here means a PC-original game that was rebuilt.
- Dedupe uses project alias plus normalized game title. A few near-duplicates may remain where lists spell titles differently.
- own_files=yes reflects list-level blanket statements, not per-project verification.
