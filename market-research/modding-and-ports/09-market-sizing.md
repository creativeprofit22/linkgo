## 1. Segments

> Segments taken from Phases 3–8 evidence (e.g. players, beginner creators,
> port players), not from assumptions.

## 2. Input Register

> Every model input: value, source URL, snapshot date, method quality, and
> low / base / high values.

## 3. Model Per Segment

> TAM = addressable people or accounts × realistic annual spend or value;
> SAM = reachable share; SOM = obtainable share. Low / base / high.

## 4. Monetisation Reality

> Evidence on what this audience pays for today (premium memberships,
> donations, tools) and how much, including the limits of a young audience's
> spending.

## 5. Third-Party Market Reports (Context Only)

> Headline figures found, with their spread and why they are not model inputs.

## 6. Sensitivity

> Which inputs move the result most, and what data would firm them up.

## 7. Verifier Report

> Every input re-checked against its source and date.

## 8. Run Log And Spend

> Request counts by command, spend against allocation, raw-file locations.
